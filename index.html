About IDR

The Institute of Digital Risk is an industry-led training and deployment institute focused on preparing professionals for digital, cyber, and AI risk roles in regulated and high-consequence environments.

Through collaboration with leading universities and industry partners, IDR combines academic insight with real-world project experience to develop the next generation of digital risk professionals.

Our Model

Academy

Bootcamps and structured training programs designed for students and professionals seeking careers in digital risk, cybersecurity and AI governance.

Innovation & Incubation

Developing new intellectual property, risk models and governance frameworks for emerging technologies such as AI and autonomous systems.

Advisory Services

Supporting organisations with implementation of recognised frameworks including NIST, ISO 27001 and emerging regulatory standards.

Our Community

IDR supports a growing community of students, early-career professionals, and industry practitioners working in sectors such as financial services, technology, and critical infrastructure.
